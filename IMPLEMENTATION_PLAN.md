# Implemented slice and design decisions

Requirements precedence: latest user adjustments → Supplement 01 → original brief. The supplement replaces the original native SwiftUI recommendation with React/TypeScript PWA. Both supplied documents were read before implementation.

1. Establish a pure TypeScript engine and configurable seed data, with explicit time/IDs and atomic UI commits.
2. Connect accomplishments to resource accounting and permanent credentials. Keep small beneficial activities distinct, with bounded rewards and explicit home-strength alternatives.
3. Add a small settlement with useful first-session construction, slow serial processing, limited passive commons, finite NPC commissions and trade.
4. Gate significant infrastructure on both crafted materials and actual category-specific accomplishments. Conceal expansion content until earned.
5. Persist versioned local snapshots, transaction audit, queue output snapshots, corrections, export/import and backups. Keep balance edits forward-only.
6. Expose local development tools behind deliberate entry, validate edits, and run projections only on cloned worlds.
7. Verify core mechanics with unit tests and real Chromium interactions; ship an offline-capable production shell.

Economic decisions: no unlimited gather button; stone work requires wood and queue time; passive food requires a garden; workshop momentum competes with expedition momentum; ore trades are limited to once per calendar day and never award gym credentials. A short walk can unlock practical woodworking, while the hall needs strength, hiking, cardio and nutrition. The home-strength track is deliberately selected, takes more qualifying days, and is not an automatic equivalence for isolated pushups. Small rewards have daily caps to avoid encouraging excessive exercise.

The starter campaign is intentionally narrow. Expansion logistics, broad optional benchmarks, more NPC arcs, stronger content tooling, and a finished art pack need subsequent playable iterations. We have not validated long-term motivation or year-scale balance merely by passing tests.
